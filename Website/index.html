Features

Everything You Need

Open Hardware

Complete CAD files, schematics, and BOM for 3D printed and machined hilt components. Compatible with standard electronics.

Custom Firmware

Arduino/ESP32 based firmware with smooth swing, gesture controls, blade styles, and sound font support. Fully configurable via web interface.

Modular Design

Swap emitters, pommels, switches, and chassis parts. Standardized connections let you mix and match components freely.

Community Driven

Built by makers for makers. Share designs, contribute code, request features, and collaborate with sabersmiths worldwide.

Proffie & CFX Support

Compatible with popular boards like Proffieboard, CFX, Golden Harvest, and more. Use your existing hardware.

Web Configurator

Browser-based configuration tool for blade styles, motion settings, and sound fonts. No drivers or software installation needed.

About

Why FreeSabers?

Commercial lightsabers cost hundreds of dollars and lock you into proprietary ecosystems. FreeSabers changes that by providing completely open designs, firmware, and tools that anyone can build, modify, and share.

Whether you're a seasoned maker with a machine shop or a beginner with a 3D printer, FreeSabers gives you the freedom to create exactly the saber you want—without licensing fees, vendor lock-in, or artificial limitations.

  • MIT Licensed — use commercially or personally
  • No vendor lock-in — standard components
  • Community governed — not corporate owned
  • Extensible architecture — build on top of it
  • Beginner friendly — comprehensive docs
  • Advanced capable — pro features included

Get Started

Build Your First Saber

01

Choose Your Path

Decide between 3D printed, machined, or hybrid construction. Browse the parts library and pick a hilt design that fits your vision.

02

Gather Components

Order electronics (board, battery, LED, speaker, switches) and print or machine your hilt parts. The BOM generator creates a complete shopping list.

03

Assemble & Wire

Follow the step-by-step assembly guide with photos and wiring diagrams. No soldering experience? We have beginner-friendly options.

04

Configure & Ignite

Connect via USB or Bluetooth, customize your blade styles and sounds in the web configurator, then ignite your creation!
